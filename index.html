Undergraduate Thesis

A Structured, Clinically-Validated Dataset for Medical Specialty Routing from Doctor–Patient Consultations

B.Sc. Thesis · RUET · 2025 – 2026

Built a patient-level tabular dataset (∼30,500 records) for symptom-to-specialty routing from real doctor–patient consultations (MedDialog corpus), spanning a 13-specialty routing taxonomy. Designed an LLM-based extraction pipeline (GPT-4.1-mini) mapping free consultation text onto a controlled 105-term symptom vocabulary, capturing clinician-annotated severity and duration alongside patient demographics. Evaluated the dataset's value through a 7-model comparison and a feature-ablation study.

Tech: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Keras/TensorFlow, OpenAI API, Jupyter
