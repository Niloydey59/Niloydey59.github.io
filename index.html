Undergraduate Thesis

A Structured, Clinically-Validated Dataset for Medical Specialty Routing from Doctor–Patient Consultations

B.Sc. Thesis · RUET · 2025 – 2026 · Supervisor: Khaled Zinnurine, Lecturer, Department of CSE, RUET

Built a patient-level dataset of about 30,500 real doctor–patient consultations (MedDialog corpus) for medical specialty routing, with a 13-specialty taxonomy derived empirically from 24 candidate classes. Designed an LLM-based pipeline (GPT-4.1-mini) with checkpointed stages for specialty classification, structured symptom extraction onto a controlled 105-term vocabulary, and severity and duration normalization. Validated the extracted data through clinician review of a stratified sample, measured with agreement statistics (Cohen's kappa, PABAK), and benchmarked 7 models with a feature-ablation study.

Tech: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Keras/TensorFlow, OpenAI API, Jupyter
